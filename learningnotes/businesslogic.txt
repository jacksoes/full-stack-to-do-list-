Business logic determines how data can be created, stored, and changed 
depending on the businesses goals.

business logic can consist of

VALIDATION - logic must meet certain criteria depending on business goals.
ex: the user must enter a valid due-date, which cannot be prior to the current date.

AUTHORIZATION - certain users have certain permissions.
ex: users can save tasks to a database, guests cannot save tasks to database only to the DOM

BUSINESS RULES - business logic that applies to a certain business domain.
ex: user cannot enter a task without a title.

DATA TRANSFORMATION - calculations performed with data.
ex: the users total is computated at checkout.

CONSISTENCY CHECKS - makes sure the data adheres to constraints.
ex: the user cannot store more than 999 tasks; if tasks > 999 then ....
